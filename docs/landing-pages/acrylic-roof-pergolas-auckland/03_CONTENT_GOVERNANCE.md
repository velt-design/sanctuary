# Content governance

This file controls accuracy, claims and publication decisions for the acrylic-roof landing page.

## Non-negotiable writing rules

Use:

- New Zealand English
- Short paragraphs
- Useful headings
- Specific explanations
- Calm, architectural language
- Confident but qualified statements

Do not use:

- Em dashes
- Keyword stuffing
- Excessive adjectives
- Fake urgency
- Discounts
- Cheap-price positioning
- Invented testimonials
- Invented customer quotations
- Unsupported superlatives
- Generic lifestyle filler

Avoid phrases such as:

- transform your outdoor living
- bring your dream to life
- luxury redefined
- perfect for every home
- best in Auckland
- highest quality

## Approved customer-language themes

These themes may be paraphrased naturally:

- Keep rain off the deck without losing too much light
- Preserve daylight into the adjoining rooms
- Retain a visual connection to the sky
- Balance light, shade and heat
- Understand clear, tinted and opal options
- Make a deck or patio usable more often
- Integrate the new structure with the house
- Create a permanent architectural result
- Understand price, drainage, consent and process

Do not present these themes as direct quotations.

## User-provided business facts approved for the draft

Subject to normal repository verification, the page may describe Sanctuary as:

- Auckland based
- A designer and installer of bespoke aluminium pergolas
- Working with mono-pitched, gable, hip and box-perimeter forms
- Creating attached and freestanding structures
- Offering clear, light grey, dark grey and opal acrylic options
- Able to consider timber sarking, insulated panels and combination roofs
- Able to consider blinds, lighting, heaters and infill panels
- Working on residential and selected commercial projects

If current repository content contradicts one of these points, report the conflict rather than silently changing the page.

## Statements allowed without numerical claims

The page may explain that:

- Clear acrylic generally retains more visual transparency than tinted or opal options.
- Darker tints generally allow less daylight through than clear options.
- Opal creates a more diffused visual effect.
- Roof depth, orientation, tint, airflow and nearby walls affect perceived comfort.
- Low-angle sun may require edge protection such as blinds.
- Open-sided structures remain exposed to wind-driven rain.
- Gutters, downpipes, roof fall, flashings and house connections are part of rainwater management.
- Price depends on the full project scope, not roof area alone.
- Consent and engineering requirements depend on the final site and design.
- Acrylic and polycarbonate are different materials.

Do not add exact performance values unless approved.

### Consent and legal wording

The final consent section must receive an internal technical review.

Do not state or imply that:

- Every acrylic pergola is exempt
- Every project requires consent
- Sanctuary can make a binding council determination
- Exempt work has no regulatory requirements

Use general wording unless a current official source and approved interpretation are supplied.

## Project-content rules

Only use project facts that can be verified from:

- Existing approved project pages
- Repository project data
- Approved internal project records
- Approved image metadata

Do not infer:

- Acrylic tint from image appearance
- Product type from a generic "clear roof" description
- Customer challenge
- Design rationale
- Consent status
- Engineering status
- Dimensions
- Completion date

If a project has incomplete proof:

- Use only verified fields
- Omit unsupported claims
- Add a code TODO
- Report the missing field at handoff

Do not show visible placeholder text in production.

## Image rules

Use:

- Existing Sanctuary project photography
- Images showing the complete structure
- Views from beneath the acrylic
- Views from inside the adjoining home
- Details showing gutters, flashing or house integration
- Images where roof tint can be identified accurately

Do not use:

- Stock photography
- AI-generated project images
- Unverified material close-ups
- Images that imply a tint different from the project record
- Customer images without approved usage rights

## Form-content rules

- Phone is required.
- Dimensions must begin blank or unknown.
- No roof form should be selected by default.
- No acrylic tint should be selected by default.
- Upload limits must match the actual implementation.
- Privacy wording must link to the current Privacy Policy.
- Do not add a response-time promise unless approved.
- Do not expose internal errors or database identifiers.

## Placeholder policy

Placeholders may exist in documentation and source-code comments.

Placeholders must not appear in the rendered production page.

If content is unapproved at implementation time:

1. Omit it cleanly.
2. Preserve the intended component structure where useful.
3. Add a source-code TODO.
4. Report it in the final implementation summary.
